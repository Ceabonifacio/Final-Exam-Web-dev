A little more purpose

A thoughtful cycle for school essentials.

CampusCycle is a student marketplace prototype for discovering useful pre-owned school supplies within your school community.

autorenew Our mission is to make school supplies easier to access and encourage students to reuse useful items.

A simple cycle

New to CampusCycle? It’s simple.

Three thoughtful steps can give a useful school supply a new start.

  1. 01 menu_book

    Browse

    Explore pre-loved textbooks, uniforms, calculators, and other supplies.

  2. 02 group

    Connect

    Inquire about an item and discuss details with its owner.

  3. 03 autorenew

    Reuse

    Give useful supplies a new home and keep the cycle going.
